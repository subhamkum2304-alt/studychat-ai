STUDYCHAT - QUICK START

1. Open server\.env and add your Gemini key:
   PORT=5000
   GEMINI_API_KEY=YOUR_REAL_KEY
   GEMINI_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash

2. Double-click start-studychat.bat

3. Keep the black window open.

4. Open http://localhost:5173

This build uses the public npm registry and contains no internal package-lock URLs.
